Pooled leave-one-session-out over the sessions that have social/news data. Every row is predicted by a model trained on the other sessions. The deliverable is a 6-component PLS model on price technicals plus sentiment levels and dynamics; the control is the same PLS on price technicals only. Forecasts are shrunk 0.30 toward zero and clipped at ±5%. The CSV keeps the legacy `ridge_price` / `ridge_sent` column names; the configured short model is PLS. With fewer than 60 sessions this is evidence that the features carry fitted weights, not a statistically robust backtest.
